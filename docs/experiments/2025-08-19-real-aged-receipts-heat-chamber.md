# Real aged receipts, heat chamber

Date: 2025-08-19
Who: Ahmed Siddiqui, Lea Fontaine
Uncertainty: U1 Small model on faded thermal paper

## Plan (written before the run)

Hypothesis: Receipts aged in a heat chamber give realistic fading the model can learn.
What changes: 3,000 receipts aged 2 to 8 weeks in a heat and UV chamber, photographed weekly.
Setup: Rented environmental chamber, 45 C, UV lamp.

## Result (written the same day)

Result: Faded thermal 86% to 91%.
Learned: Real ageing helps a lot. Contrast, not shape, is what the model loses.
Outcome: Partly worked
